Kueue operator installation started.

1. Wait for the operator to reach "Succeeded":

   watch "oc get csv -n {{ .Values.kueue.namespace }} | grep kueue"

2. Verify the Kueue controller is running:

   oc get pods -n {{ .Values.kueue.namespace }}

3. Now configure Kueue resources manually (not managed by this chart):

   - ResourceFlavors      (cluster-scoped)
   - ClusterQueues         (cluster-scoped)
   - LocalQueues           (namespace-scoped)
   - WorkloadPriorityClasses (cluster-scoped)
